# AgentStock AI — Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

## 1. Scope & Permitted Use
AgentStock AI is strictly licensed for **legitimate commercial inventory management, supply chain forecasting, shelf stock reconciliation, and supplier purchase order automation**.

## 2. Prohibited Uses
Users and organizations are strictly prohibited from utilizing AgentStock AI for:
1. **Surveillance & Tracking**: Monitoring retail workers, tracking shoppers, or performing biometric analysis.
2. **Illicit Goods**: Managing, counting, or facilitating the purchase of illegal narcotics, unregulated firearms, weapons, explosives, or contraband.
3. **Financial Fraud**: Creating fictitious purchase orders, falsified invoices, tax evasion records, or deceitful commercial claims.
4. **Harassment & Spam**: Dispatched bulk unsolicited communications to suppliers via WhatsApp, Email, or automated phone dialing.
5. **System Exploitation**: Attempting prompt injections, jailbreaks, denial-of-service, or unauthorized multi-tenant data access.

## 3. Enforcement & Account Suspension
Violations of this Acceptable Use Policy result in immediate account suspension, cancellation of subscriptions without refund, and potential notification to relevant commercial law enforcement authorities.
