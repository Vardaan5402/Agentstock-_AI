# AgentStock AI — Privacy & Biometric Protection Policy

## 1. No Facial Recognition or Biometric Tracking
AgentStock AI is designed exclusively for physical merchandise, cartons, shelves, and barcodes.
We do NOT implement or utilize facial recognition, emotion analysis, employee surveillance, or biometric profiling.

## 2. Privacy Person Filter
Camera frames captured via mobile device or webcam are automatically inspected by the `PrivacyPersonFilter`.
If a person or face is detected within the frame:
1. The frame is flagged with a `PRIVACY_FILTER` warning.
2. Visual item counting is disabled.
3. The user is prompted to re-aim the camera exclusively at inventory items.

## 3. Data Ownership & Portability (GDPR / CCPA)
- **Export Rights**: Users may download their complete business catalog, suppliers, and purchase history at any time in standard JSON format.
- **Right to Erasure**: Users can permanently delete their business records and account data directly through the self-service privacy portal.
